房神 HouseTycoon 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 8 月 8 日

重點：房神不需要帳號，不含廣告或分析 SDK，也沒有開發者營運的資料伺服器。收藏、模擬資產、設定與定位結果保存在您的裝置上。

App 在裝置上處理的資料

  • 位置：經您允許後，App 使用目前位置顯示附近建案。位置用於裝置上的距離計算與地圖顯示，不會傳送到開發者的伺服器。拒絕定位後仍可依縣市、行政區或地址使用主要功能。
  • 遊戲與偏好：虛擬現金、模擬持有物件、收藏、篩選條件與情境設定儲存在裝置本機。
  • 快取：為改善載入速度，App 可能在裝置上暫存建案圖片。系統會依容量限制自動移除快取。

外部網站、圖片與 Apple 地圖

App 會直接向畫面上標示的建商、代銷、建築或房產資訊來源載入建案圖片。您點擊來源連結時，網站會在 Apple 提供的瀏覽器畫面開啟。這些第三方主機可能依其政策接收一般網路連線資訊，例如 IP 位址、裝置瀏覽器資訊、請求時間與所要求的圖片或頁面。房神的開發者不會取得或保存這些主機的連線紀錄。

地圖功能使用 Apple MapKit。Apple 可能依其隱私權政策處理地圖服務所需的請求與位置資訊。詳情請參閱 Apple 隱私權政策。

我們不蒐集的資料

房神不要求姓名、電話、電子郵件、帳號、聯絡人、照片、付款資料或身分證件；不含第三方廣告或跨 App 追蹤；不出售或出租個人資料；也不把您的模擬交易與收藏上傳給開發者。

保存、刪除與安全

本機遊戲資料會保存到您使用 App 內的重置功能或移除 App 為止。圖片快取由 iOS 與 App 的容量限制管理。因為開發者沒有持有您的伺服器端帳號資料，所以沒有可由開發者匯出或刪除的雲端副本。裝置資料的安全性也取決於您的裝置密碼、Apple 帳號與作業系統設定。

兒童、政策更新與聯絡方式

本 App 不以兒童為主要對象，也不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。若功能或法律要求改變，我們會更新本頁並修改最後更新日期。隱私問題請寄至 tel0955850213@yahoo.com.tw，或前往支援頁面。

Privacy Policy for HouseTycoon

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Summary: HouseTycoon requires no account, contains no advertising or analytics SDK, and operates no developer-controlled data server. Favorites, simulation holdings, settings, and location results remain on your device.

Data Processed on Your Device

  • Location: With permission, the App uses your current location to show nearby developments. Location is used for on-device distance calculations and map presentation and is not sent to the developer. The main features remain available through city, district, and address search if permission is denied.
  • Game data and preferences: Virtual cash, simulated holdings, favorites, filters, and scenario settings are stored locally.
  • Cache: The App may temporarily cache development images on your device. Cached files are removed under the configured storage limit.

External Sites, Images, and Apple Maps

The App loads development images directly from the labeled developer, sales agency, architecture, or real-estate information source. Opening a source link uses an Apple-provided browser view. Those third-party hosts may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address, browser information, request time, and the requested image or page under their own policies. The HouseTycoon developer does not receive or retain those server logs.

Map features use Apple MapKit. Apple may process requests and location information needed to provide maps under the Apple Privacy Policy.

What We Do Not Collect

HouseTycoon does not request your name, phone number, email address, account, contacts, photos, payment details, or identity documents. It contains no third-party ads or cross-app tracking, does not sell personal data, and does not upload your simulated transactions or favorites to the developer.

Retention, Deletion, Security, and Contact

Local game data remains until you use the in-app reset action or remove the App. Image cache is managed by iOS and the App's storage limit. Because the developer holds no server-side account data, there is no developer-held cloud copy to export or delete.
